Ayah — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Ayah is a Quran reading, listening and memorization app by Lujane Yaffa. It has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it contains no third-party advertising or analytics code. This page explains exactly what the app stores and where it goes.

What stays on your device

The ayahs you mark as memorized, your reading place, and your settings (reciter, speed, display options) are saved in the app's storage on your device. If you never make an account, that is where they stay.

Microphone (memorization check)

The memorization check listens to you recite so it can compare what you say with the page. The speech recognition model runs entirely on your device. Your voice is never recorded to a file, uploaded, or shared with anyone, and the app only listens while you have tapped Start Reciting.

Optional account

You can optionally create an account (a username and a 6–12 digit PIN — no email, name or phone number) to back up your memorized ayahs and keep them in sync across your devices. If you do, we store:

  • your username;
  • a scrambled (salted, hashed) version of your PIN — the PIN itself cannot be read back or recovered;
  • your synced data: the list of memorized ayahs, your reading place, and your settings.

This is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Cloudflare Workers and D1). It is used only to provide sync. It is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used to track you. A very short-lived record of failed login attempts (by username and network address) is kept to protect accounts from PIN guessing.

Deleting your account: open Account in the app, enter your PIN and tap Delete account. Your username, PIN hash and synced data are permanently removed from our servers. Data saved on your device stays on your device until you delete the app.

Other services the app contacts

  • Quran.com (api.quran.com, verses.quran.com): supplies verse text, translation, tafsir and recitation audio. When you read or play an ayah, your device requests it from them, so they receive your network address and which ayah was requested, under their own privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net): serves the open-source library used by the memorization check. Your device downloads it when you first use that feature.

Children

Ayah does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only information the app can hold is a username, a hashed PIN and synced Quran progress.
